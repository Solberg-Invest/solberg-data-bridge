# Solberg Data Bridge

Use the Solberg data you choose to share in Claude chat, Cowork, or Claude Code. Ask about your accounts, compare holdings, check data freshness, or turn saved records into a table or export. With separate authorization and active Terminal access, you can also read published research and analysts’ portfolios.

Financial Profile management lets Claude read your complete profile and accounts, update answers, and add, edit or remove manual assets and liabilities. You can start with an empty profile. Existing read-only connections retain their selected sharing until you authorize management. Connected balances remain provider-managed; tools do not trade or transfer money. You can pause or disconnect access in Solberg. Missing values and stale observations remain identified.

## Connect

1. Install the Solberg Data Bridge plugin. In Claude chat or Cowork, open its Connectors tab and connect Solberg. In Claude Code, use `/mcp` to authenticate the bundled connection.
2. Sign in to your own Solberg account and approve Financial Profile management. You can fill in and maintain your profile in the conversation.
3. Ask “How current is my shared Solberg data?” to check the connection.

One package works across all three surfaces. Each user authorizes their own account. Never paste passwords, access tokens, or exchange credentials into a conversation.
